## Why

Work that belongs to another department dead-ends in an answer, and the asker starts again there.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An answer that matches another space's request category offers **Hand off**, drafting the category, text and the cites the asker ticks
- [ ] Nothing reaches the department until the asker sends it
- [ ] The receiving steward sees only cites they can open; the rest are counted
