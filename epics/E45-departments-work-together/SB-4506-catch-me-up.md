## Why

Someone back from leave or new to a project reads weeks of changes to find what matters.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Catch me up** on a space, branch or collection since a date or the last visit, at most 90 days, gives one cited summary
- [ ] A document the person has since lost is left out
- [ ] It's offered once on the inbox after a notification pause ends
