## Why

An answer can cite a decision that a later one replaced, and nothing says so.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A decision can name the one it replaces, in front matter or when the steward confirms
- [ ] A cite of a replaced decision shows the newer one if the asker can open it
- [ ] A topic shows its trail of decisions; a suggested replacement waits for the steward
