## Why

A note's readers are a snapshot of the space's members when it was published; when the space narrows, the note doesn't.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A steward's review list (SB-1709) includes notes whose readers include someone no longer a member of the space
- [ ] Each is checked live as the steward and listed with the number of readers to remove, never their names
- [ ] Republishing the note narrowed takes it off the list
