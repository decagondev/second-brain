## Why

Weekly updates are written by hand from what the brain already knows.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A space with `updates: true` gets a draft update each Monday from the `update` agent: what changed, new decisions and goals' status, each line cited
- [ ] The steward edits and publishes it as a note under `notes/SPACE/updates/`
- [ ] The parent's Start tab lists its children's latest published updates, each checked as the reader
