## Why

A space's reading list can point new members at gone or old documents.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The review list shows `start_here` items that are gone, stale, or that more than a quarter of the space's members can't open, each with why
- [ ] An item the steward can't open is a count only
- [ ] A deleted item is listed; a fine one isn't
