## Why

A draft that's nearly right can only be published as is, edited by hand or thrown away.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Ask for changes** with a note of at most 500 characters redrafts from the same sources and shows the diff
- [ ] At most 3 rounds; a fourth is refused
- [ ] The redraft cites nothing the first draft couldn't; the note is never a source
