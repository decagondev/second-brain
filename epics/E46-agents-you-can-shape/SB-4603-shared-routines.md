## Why

Every member of a team sets up the same routine by hand, or doesn't.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A steward shares a routine with the space and members opt in
- [ ] Each run is a separate ask as each member, sent to them alone; the steward sees only how many opted in
- [ ] It counts toward each member's 5 routines and stops for a member who leaves
