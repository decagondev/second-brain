## Why

A new joiner's first weeks depend on a manager remembering what to send and when.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The `onboarding` rhythm starts on SCIM add or by a steward, and every item is one the joiner can open
- [ ] Unanswered questions reach stewards as gaps without the joiner's name unless they agree
- [ ] `onboarding: false` turns it off for a space; leaving the space stops it
