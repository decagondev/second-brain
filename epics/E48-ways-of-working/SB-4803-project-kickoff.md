## Why

Each project starts from a blank page, and nobody finds what similar projects decided.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Start a project** creates a collection, a goal draft and a decision log, and drafts a kickoff brief of up to 5 similar past projects
- [ ] The kickoff brief cites only what the lead can open
- [ ] A member from another space is added only after their steward approves, and sees only what they could already open

## Subtasks

- [ ] Update the man pages listed above first (manual first, P2)
- [ ] Start a project
- [ ] Kickoff brief agent and eval suite
- [ ] Cross-space members
- [ ] Tests
- [ ] Write or extend the law tests
- [ ] Devlog entry, ledger and changelog updated
