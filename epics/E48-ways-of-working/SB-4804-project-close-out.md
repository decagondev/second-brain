## Why

What a project learned leaves with the people who ran it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Close project** drafts a retrospective (goals, decisions, open waits, lessons) for the lead, every line cited
- [ ] A lesson becomes a note only when a steward publishes it, and the next kickoff brief finds it
- [ ] A closed collection is archived read-only; nothing is deleted

## Subtasks

- [ ] Update the man pages listed above first (manual first, P2)
- [ ] Retrospective agent and eval suite
- [ ] Lessons as notes
- [ ] Archive
- [ ] Tests
- [ ] Write or extend the law tests
- [ ] Devlog entry, ledger and changelog updated
