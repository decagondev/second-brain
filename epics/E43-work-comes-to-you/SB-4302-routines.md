## Why

People ask the same question every Monday by hand.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A saved question can become a routine, daily on working mornings or weekly, at most 5 per person
- [ ] The notify job asks it as the person and sends the cited answer, what changed first, to their chat or email
- [ ] A routine pauses at the person's space budget and stops when they are deprovisioned
