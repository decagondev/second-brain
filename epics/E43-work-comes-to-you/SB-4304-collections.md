## Why

The documents for a piece of work are scattered, and each ask has to find them again.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A collection names up to 50 documents and saved threads, shared with named people, stored as ids only
- [ ] **Ask this collection** scopes an ask to its documents; it cites nothing outside them
- [ ] Each reader sees only the items they can open; others are counted, not named
