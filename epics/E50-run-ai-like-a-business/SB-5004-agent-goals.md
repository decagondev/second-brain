## Why

We measure each agent, but nobody says what good looks like for it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An owner sets a one-sentence `job:` and up to 3 targets on value numbers only
- [ ] The registry shows each target against the last 4 weeks, and a miss two weeks running tells the owner once
- [ ] The agent's record lists every target and its history; meeting a target changes nothing else
