## Why

When an owner leaves, their documents show owner gone but nobody takes them over.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A steward names an active member of the space as caretaker of an owner-gone document; one record, audited
- [ ] Who to ask and the owners' note use the caretaker until the source names an active owner
- [ ] A deprovisioned caretaker is dropped
