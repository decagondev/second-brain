## Why

A published note doesn't say which agent drafted it, who asked, who edited it and who approved it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A published note's front matter and history name the agent, the person who asked, each editor and the approver
- [ ] The passage view and the note's page show them
- [ ] History matches the audit log, and imported marks are kept
