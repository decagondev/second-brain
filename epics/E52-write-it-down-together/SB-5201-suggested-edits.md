## Why

The brain finds a source stale or wrong, but can only tell the owner, not hand them a fix.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A stale, contradicted, replaced or reported source gets a drafted suggested edit for its owner: the passage, the change and the supporting cites
- [ ] Nothing is written through a connector; the owner applies it in the source tool or dismisses it with a reason
- [ ] A dismissed suggestion isn't made again for the same passage
