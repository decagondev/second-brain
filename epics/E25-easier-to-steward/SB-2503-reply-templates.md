## Why

Stewards type the same replies again and again.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each space keeps up to 20 replies (a name and up to 2,000 characters) its stewards write and edit; Use a template fills the editable reply box
- [ ] Templates are never sent to the model
- [ ] A steward of another space never sees them
