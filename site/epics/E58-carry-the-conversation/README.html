E58: Carry the Conversation

A conversation follows a person from a chat thread to the web, to their other devices and to a colleague, and work they left unfinished finds them again.

Waves: W57 · Tickets: 7 · Stories: 2

Planned 2026-11-27 after W55, with the user. The concept is in 23-place-carry-reach.md. Every ticket keeps to the rules there: no new service the cell must run, no new store, no new third-party Go module, at most one setting a feature, no new page, nothing widens what anyone sees, a person approves every write, the tree only narrows, agents are definitions in the release bundle, stored results are checked when read, every long run has a ceiling, one level of consultation, the AI label never comes off by itself, a rhythm only drafts and stops with its subject, a brief about a person draws only on what they published, lent access lasts one run and travels with its result, a label never drops while the brain never changes who can open a source, and a model call stays in its region while a new vendor is an adapter against a recorded fake.

User stories

  • US-58.1: As anyone, I want to follow up in a chat thread, continue on the web, keep my conversations on every device and share a whole conversation, so that a conversation isn't stuck where it started. Delivered by SB-5801, SB-5802, SB-5803, SB-5804.
  • US-58.2: As anyone or a steward, I want my open things shown when I start again, a reaction that saves a thread, and skills over MCP, so that unfinished work and useful threads aren't lost. Delivered by SB-5805, SB-5806, SB-5807.

Exit gate

  • A follow-up in a chat thread continues the conversation as the person who replied, and only theirs
  • A conversation continued on the web, on another device or by a colleague is checked again as that reader, answer by answer
  • Synced conversations are the person's own: off by default, gone with forget, listed by nobody else
  • Every law test still green; no new store, service or Go module

Decisions for the user

Approved by the user on 2026-11-27 (23-place-carry-reach.md), every recommended decision: conversations follow a person to every device only if they turn it on, holding questions, kept and dropped documents and trace ids for 30 days, gone with forget (SB-5803); one reaction (default brain) offers to save a thread, and nothing is read or saved until the person confirms in a DM (SB-5806).
