E08: Factory and Hand-off

One factory produces and runs every tenant's Second Brain, and can hand custody of one to its company without a rebuild.

Waves: W1, W7 · Tickets: 11 · Stories: 5

User stories

  • US-08.1: As a parent-org platform lead, I want every cell to carry a custody record and a placement from day one, so that hand-off later is a workflow, not a migration. Delivered by SB-114, SB-701, SB-702.
  • US-08.2: As a company owner, I want our Second Brain moved into our own cloud account under our own key, so that we hold custody of our data. Delivered by SB-703, SB-704, SB-705.
  • US-08.3: As a company security lead, I want proof that the factory keeps no access we didn't agree to, so that custody means what it says. Delivered by SB-706.
  • US-08.4: As a company IT operator, I want to keep receiving signed releases after hand-off, so that we stay patched without a support contract per release. Delivered by SB-707.
  • US-08.5: As a company owner after divestiture, I want a complete export so we can leave the factory, so that a sale doesn't strand our knowledge. Delivered by SB-708, SB-709, SB-710.
