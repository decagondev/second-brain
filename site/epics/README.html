How to pick up work: take the lowest open wave and slice, check its dependencies are done, then follow the ticket's subtasks top to bottom. The first subtask is always the manual.

Epics

Epic Goal Waves Tickets
E00 Manual, Laws and Proofs The spec, the laws and the first formal models exist before features, and CI enforces the process. W0, W8 22
E01 Tenancy and Identity One manifest and one command make an isolated cell; users sign in with their own SSO and see only their tenant. W1 8
E02 Policy Kernel Every decision about who may see or do what goes through one small, analysable policy engine that treats unknown as deny. W1 4
E03 Connectors and Ingestion Sources flow in read-only through a four-call contract, with ACLs, provenance and freshness, and ingested text is treated as untrusted data. W2, W6 12
E04 Retrieval and Cited Answers Users ask in plain language and get answers citing document versions they can open, or a clear uncited label. W3 10
E05 Actions, Approvals, Audit and Kill Switches Agents draft and propose; humans send and approve; every tool call is audited before it runs; anything can be switched off in seconds. W4 12
E06 Evals, Release and Supply Chain No agent, prompt, policy or model change reaches a tenant without passing evals, and every running build is a signed build that passed the gate. W0, W3, W5 12
E07 Roll-out, Admin and Operations A company onboards itself with one file and three commands, and operators can see and control every tenant. W6 12
E08 Factory and Hand-off One factory produces and runs every tenant's Second Brain, and can hand custody of one to its company without a rebuild. W1, W7 11
E09 Cells on GCP A real cell runs on Google Cloud in its own project, with its schedules, metrics and verifier, and nothing in the core knows it's GCP. W9 8
E10 Department Spaces A company's brain holds all its knowledge, split into department spaces that can only narrow what each source allows. W10 8
E11 Department Templates and Connectors Every common department starts from a template with its sources, labels, agents and evals, including code, HR and accounting systems. W11 7
E12 Space Governance Department leads run their own spaces; barriers, segregation of duties, access reviews and reorgs work the way a real organisation needs. W12 8
E13 Portfolio Department Grants If the parent reads company data at all, it gets consented, aggregate-only, revocable grants on named spaces, never a superuser. Parked: Q-01 stays no (2026-11-20). parked 4
E14 Hardening Before calling it feature complete: review, fuzz, tidy, reconcile the manual with the code, and close coverage gaps. W13 5
E15 Cell Runtime Every part of a cell runs together as real services, from one configuration to a whole cell end to end. W14 9
E16 Self-hosted Cell A whole cell on one machine the company owns, in containers that mirror GCP, plus four easy wins. W15 9
E17 Team Knowledge Ten small features that let a team hand knowledge to each other and keep it current, on one sovereign machine. W16 10
E18 Easy to Run IT runs, upgrades, restores and gets help for a cell with one command each, on one machine or two VMs. W17 8
E19 Protect the Data Nothing readable leaves the box, the keys survive the box, and data-protection duties are met from the console. W18 7
E20 Close the Loops W16's features work everywhere, and the brain reads Word files and answers in Mattermost. W19 5
E21 Keep People in the Loop People hear when a question, a reply or a source needs them, and nothing they hear says more than they could open. W20 6
E22 Keep Knowledge Owned Documents keep an owner, copies cite once, the brain learns from wrong citations, and a hidden source can be asked for. W21 7
E23 Fit the Site The UI in English, German and French, audit to the SIEM, off-box copies to S3, fair on small boxes, tokens and email. W22 7
E24 Easier to Ask An answer is easy to take away, and no screen leaves the asker without a next step. W23 6
E25 Easier to Steward A steward clears their list in minutes and doesn't repeat work, and nobody is pinged while they are away. W24 7
E26 Easier to Operate Whoever runs the box sets everything from one file, checks each channel first, and is told how to fix what is wrong. W25 7
