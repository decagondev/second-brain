Epics

Epic Goal Waves Tickets
E00 Manual, Laws and Proofs The spec, the laws and the first formal models exist before features, and CI enforces the process. W0 15
E01 Tenancy and Identity One manifest and one command make an isolated cell; users sign in with their own SSO and see only their tenant. W1 8
E02 Policy Kernel Every decision about who may see or do what goes through one small, analysable policy engine that treats unknown as deny. W1 4
E03 Connectors and Ingestion Sources flow in read-only through a four-call contract, with ACLs, provenance and freshness, and ingested text is treated as untrusted data. W2, W6 12
E04 Retrieval and Cited Answers Users ask in plain language and get answers citing document versions they can open, or a clear uncited label. W3 10
E05 Actions, Approvals, Audit and Kill Switches Agents draft and propose; humans send and approve; every tool call is audited before it runs; anything can be switched off in seconds. W4 12
E06 Evals, Release and Supply Chain No agent, prompt, policy or model change reaches a tenant without passing evals, and every running build is a signed build that passed the gate. W0, W3, W5 12
E07 Roll-out, Admin and Operations A company onboards itself with one file and three commands, and operators can see and control every tenant. W6 12
E08 Factory and Hand-off One factory produces and runs every tenant's Second Brain, and can hand custody of one to its company without a rebuild. W1, W7 11
