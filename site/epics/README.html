Plan: epics and tickets

60 epics, 163 user stories and 451 tickets across 59 waves. W8 to W12 add department brains, and W13 hardens everything (08-department-brains.md). The narrative, wave exit gates and slices are in the PRD.

How to pick up work: take the lowest open wave and slice, check its dependencies are done, then follow the ticket's subtasks top to bottom. The first subtask is always the manual.

E00 Manual, Laws and Proofs The spec, the laws and the first formal models exist before features, and CI enforces the process. W0, W8 22
E01 Tenancy and Identity One manifest and one command make an isolated cell; users sign in with their own SSO and see only their tenant. W1 8
E02 Policy Kernel Every decision about who may see or do what goes through one small, analysable policy engine that treats unknown as deny. W1 4
E03 Connectors and Ingestion Sources flow in read-only through a four-call contract, with ACLs, provenance and freshness, and ingested text is treated as untrusted data. W2, W6 12
E04 Retrieval and Cited Answers Users ask in plain language and get answers citing document versions they can open, or a clear uncited label. W3 10
E05 Actions, Approvals, Audit and Kill Switches Agents draft and propose; humans send and approve; every tool call is audited before it runs; anything can be switched off in seconds. W4 12
E06 Evals, Release and Supply Chain No agent, prompt, policy or model change reaches a tenant without passing evals, and every running build is a signed build that passed the gate. W0, W3, W5 12
E07 Roll-out, Admin and Operations A company onboards itself with one file and three commands, and operators can see and control every tenant. W6 12
E08 Factory and Hand-off One factory produces and runs every tenant's Second Brain, and can hand custody of one to its company without a rebuild. W1, W7 11
E09 Cells on GCP A real cell runs on Google Cloud in its own project, with its schedules, metrics and verifier, and nothing in the core knows it's GCP. W9 8
E10 Department Spaces A company's brain holds all its knowledge, split into department spaces that can only narrow what each source allows. W10 8
E11 Department Templates and Connectors Every common department starts from a template with its sources, labels, agents and evals, including code, HR and accounting systems. W11 7
E12 Space Governance Department leads run their own spaces; barriers, segregation of duties, access reviews and reorgs work the way a real organisation needs. W12 8
E13 Portfolio Department Grants If the parent reads company data at all, it gets consented, aggregate-only, revocable grants on named spaces, never a superuser. Parked: Q-01 stays no (2026-11-20). parked 4
E14 Hardening Before calling it feature complete: review, fuzz, tidy, reconcile the manual with the code, and close coverage gaps. W13 5
E15 Cell Runtime Every part of a cell runs together as real services, from one configuration to a whole cell end to end. W14 9
E16 Self-hosted Cell A whole cell on one machine the company owns, in containers that mirror GCP, plus four easy wins. W15 9
E17 Team Knowledge Ten small features that let a team hand knowledge to each other and keep it current, on one sovereign machine. W16 10
E18 Easy to Run IT runs, upgrades, restores and gets help for a cell with one command each, on one machine or two VMs. W17 8
E19 Protect the Data Nothing readable leaves the box, the keys survive the box, and data-protection duties are met from the console. W18 7
E20 Close the Loops W16's features work everywhere, and the brain reads Word files and answers in Mattermost. W19 5
E21 Keep People in the Loop People hear when a question, a reply or a source needs them, and nothing they hear says more than they could open. W20 6
E22 Keep Knowledge Owned Documents keep an owner, copies cite once, the brain learns from wrong citations, and a hidden source can be asked for. W21 7
E23 Fit the Site The UI in English, German and French, audit to the SIEM, off-box copies to S3, fair on small boxes, tokens and email. W22 7
E24 Easier to Ask An answer is easy to take away, and no screen leaves the asker without a next step. W23 6
E25 Easier to Steward A steward clears their list in minutes and doesn't repeat work, and nobody is pinged while they are away. W24 7
E26 Easier to Operate Whoever runs the box sets everything from one file, checks each channel first, and is told how to fix what is wrong. W25 7
E27 Fresh Answers An asker can tell at a glance when an answer rests on old sources, and the brain prefers the newer of two equal ones. W26 6
E28 A Tidy Corpus Stewards and owners can see what has gone stale or unused, and take it out of answers without waiting on the source. W27 7
E29 A Focused Context What the model reads stays short and on topic, and the operator can see when the index, the scopes, the eval cases or the prompts start to creep. W28 7
E30 Show the Evidence An asker can check an answer without leaving it, and the answer reads in their language. W29 6
E31 Fill the Gaps Stewards see what people couldn't find, and the upkeep is spread across stewards and owners. W30 7
E32 Run Without Surprises The operator learns about trouble from the cell before users do, and can fix the small things in one command. W31 7
E33 Ask Better An asker gets more out of each answer and knows what to ask. W32 7
E34 Capture Knowledge A good answer, once given, is kept for the next person, and every document keeps someone looking after it. W33 7
E35 Trust and Control People can see and remove what the brain keeps about them, and admins see access and spending coming. W34 6
E36 Trust Each Answer An asker knows how far to trust an answer, and a steward can fix one that was wrong. W35 7
E37 Capture Conversations What people decide in meetings and chat reaches the brain, after a steward's click. W36 7
E38 Work Where People Are The brain is in reach from wherever people work, and admins see how it's used. W37 6
E39 One Org, Many Levels Spaces follow the company's shape (company, department, team), and access only narrows down the tree. W38 7
E40 An Agent for Every Department Every department template has an agent worth asking, agents can run on a schedule or when something happens, and admins see every agent in one place. W39 7
E41 Run the Company The work around knowledge (goals, updates, policies, requests and budgets) lives where the knowledge is, without becoming a second project tool. W40 6
E42 Agents That Do the Legwork Agents prepare, research and follow up for people, and every result is still a cited draft or a message to the person themselves. W41 7
E43 Work That Comes to You Each person sees in one place what waits on them, has recurring questions answered for them, and keeps context for their work, all checked as them. W42 7
E44 Govern Every Agent Every agent has an owner, real feedback improves its evals, the gate catches a bad definition, and admins see the cost and the risk. W43 6
E45 Departments Work Together A question or a piece of work that spans departments reaches the right ones, as the asker, and leaders see the week across their branch without assembling it. W44 7
E46 Agents You Can Shape Stewards and owners shape what agents produce, without building agents in the browser, and anyone can see how an answer or draft was made. W45 6
E47 Every Agent Accountable Every agent's use is measured, any steward can stop it where it speaks, its cost is capped, its changes are tried before they ship, and its record can be handed to an auditor. W46 7
E48 Ways of Working The company's recurring moments (a new joiner, a project, a quarter, a reorg, a 1:1) start the right drafts at the right time, without a steward remembering each step. W47 7
E49 Agents That Check With You Agents ask before they guess, take comments where they belong, show a plan before a long run, and notice when their sources change. W48 6
E50 Run AI Like a Business An admin sees what AI is used for and what it costs in one place, chooses how much model each task deserves, limits a person as well as an agent, gives agents targets, and learns whether a change actually helped. W49 7
E51 Guard What Goes In Nothing a source says can steer an agent or leak through it, a new feature reaches a pilot before everyone, and anyone can see how an agent changed. W50 7
E52 Write It Down Together What the brain learns flows back to the sources, drafts are written together, and every answer points to exactly where it came from. W51 7
E53 Agents That Act Carefully Agents start on the work events that matter, can be tried before they're turned on, can borrow access for one run with a named lender, and draft the next action for a person to send. W52 6
E54 Know Who Can See A document shared too widely is noticed and held back before the brain spreads it, its label goes wherever its content goes, and everyone can see how far each agent reaches. W53 7
E55 See and Steer the Context A person sees what each answer was built from, can change it, and can find out why something they expected wasn't used. W54 7
E56 Keep Answering, Spend Less A provider failing doesn't stop the brain, and the same quality costs less. W55 6
E57 Right Model, Right Place Every call goes to the cheapest gated model that fits it, in the region the company allows, people come first when a provider is busy, and work that can wait is batched on every provider without holding a run open. W56 6
E58 Carry the Conversation A conversation follows a person from a chat thread to the web, to their other devices and to a colleague, and work they left unfinished finds them again. W57 7
E59 Reach Microsoft 365 A company on Microsoft 365 gets the same brain as one on Google Workspace: documents with their sensitivity labels, groups behind every check, Teams as a place to ask, and calendars, transcripts and shared mailboxes feeding the same agents. W58 7
