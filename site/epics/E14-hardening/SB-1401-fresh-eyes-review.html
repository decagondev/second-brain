Why

W8 to W12 went in fast; a review by someone who didn't write it finds what the author can't.

Acceptance criteria

  • ✓An independent review of every change from W8 to W12, the department brains, against the laws and the manual
  • ✓Every finding is fixed, or recorded with a reason in the findings register or the bug log
  • ✓Every fixed bug has a regression test that failed first

Subtasks

  • ✓Update the man pages the change touches first (manual first, P2)
  • ✓Review
  • ✓Fix what it finds
  • ✓Regression tests
  • ✓Devlog entry, ledger and changelog updated

Progress

2026-11-20: three independent reviews (the authorisation path; governance and data lifecycle; connectors, ingest and the GCP runtime) reported 2 critical and about 30 high and medium findings. S13.1a fixed both critical ones and the misleading diff: PT-0005 (secrets split across chunks, missed forms; BUG-0014) and PT-0006/PT-0007 (steward values that read back differently, the diff hiding a change; BUG-0015), each with a regression test. S13.1b fixed the authorisation and approval findings: PT-0008 (BUG-0016) and PT-0009 (BUG-0017), each case with a regression test. S13.1c fixed the governance findings: PT-0010 (BUG-0018); PT-0011 (holds and reporting lines depend on the cell runtime's wiring) is accepted until the runtime exists. S13.1d fixed the ingest, connector and runtime findings: PT-0012 (BUG-0019) and PT-0013 (BUG-0020); PT-0014 (whole-second cursors, opt-in backfill, report versions) was accepted as a follow-up and fixed the same day (S13.6). Every critical and high finding is fixed with a regression test reproducing the reviewer's scenario; the medium ones are fixed or recorded with a reason.
