E27: Fresh Answers

An asker can tell at a glance when an answer rests on old sources, and the brain prefers the newer of two equal ones.

Waves: W26 · Tickets: 6 · Stories: 2

Proposed 2026-11-23 after W25 and accepted by the user the same day, with every recommended decision. The concept is in 13-fresh-tidy-focused.md. Every ticket keeps to the bloat rules there: no new service the cell must run, no new store, no new third-party Go module, at most one setting a feature, no new page, and nothing widens what anyone sees.

User stories

  • US-27.1: As an asker, I want to be warned when an answer rests on old or unsynced sources, and to report one that is outdated, so that I don't rely on something that moved. Delivered by SB-2701, SB-2702, SB-2703, SB-2704.
  • US-27.2: As a steward, I want a document's own review date honoured, and newer documents preferred in a tie, so that the brain's idea of fresh matches ours. Delivered by SB-2705, SB-2706.

Slices

Exit gate

  • ✓An answer resting mostly on stale cites warns, and a hidden stale document changes nothing
  • ✓A reviewed cite isn't stale until its review lapses
  • ✓An outdated report lists the document, and a mark clears it
  • ✓A connector past its freshness target is named on the answer; an unknown sync time is never shown as fresh
  • ✓A date in a document only makes it stale sooner
  • ✓Every law test still green; no new store, service or Go module

Decisions for the user

Decided 2026-11-23 (13-fresh-tidy-focused.md): all of W26 to W28, in order; warn the asker when more than half of an answer's cites are stale (SB-2701); stewards retire a document from their own space's answers only, undoable and audited (SB-2801); owners' monthly note on by default, each person can turn it off (SB-2806); conversations send the last 8 questions (SB-2901).
