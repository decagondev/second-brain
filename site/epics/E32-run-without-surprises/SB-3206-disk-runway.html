SB-3206: Disk runway

Status Done

Why

Nobody sees the disk filling until it is full.

Acceptance criteria

  • ✓The host step keeps 14 days of the data disk's used bytes; the doctor and the console estimate days until full, warn under 30 and alert under 7
  • ✓Fewer than 3 days of history shows not enough history, never a number
  • ✓Steady growth gives the right estimate; a shrinking disk shows no runway

Subtasks

  • ✓Update the man pages listed above first (manual first, P2)
  • ✓Host step
  • ✓Doctor and console
  • ✓Tests
  • ✓Write or extend the law tests
  • ✓Devlog entry, ledger and changelog updated

Progress

box.Runway fits the used-bytes trend from state/box/disk.json (at most one sample a day, kept by wire.withDiskHistory, read by facts.sh); the disk runway line passes with not enough history yet or not growing, warns under 30 days and fails under 7, and its fix names docs/runbooks/selfhost.md#disk-runway. Test: box/runway_test.go.
