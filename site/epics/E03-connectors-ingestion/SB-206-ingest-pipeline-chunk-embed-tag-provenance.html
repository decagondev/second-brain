SB-206: Ingest pipeline: chunk, embed, tag provenance

Status Done

Why

Every chunk must know where it came from and which version it is.

Acceptance criteria

  • ✓Each chunk has tenant, connector, source id, version, ACL prefilter and provenance hash
  • ✓Pipeline is idempotent per (source id, version)

Subtasks

  • ✓Update the man pages listed above first (manual first, P2)
  • ✓Queue consumer (Pipeline.Sync consumes the change feed; the durable per-connector cursor is SB-208)
  • ✓Chunker with stable boundaries
  • ✓Embedding adapter behind port
  • ✓Provenance record
  • ✓Write or extend the law tests: LAW-13
  • ✓Devlog entry, ledger and changelog updated

Progress

2026-11-19: done. - internal/core/ingest: Normalise and Chunks (paragraphs packed to 1,200 characters, long paragraphs cut at a space; boundaries depend only on the text); Cite and a derived ChunkID; Pipeline.Ingest, which skips an indexed version that is the same or newer and replaces all of a document's chunks in one write; Sync, where the last change per document wins, deletes are removed and permission changes are forced; and Rebuild, which drops the index and replays from the start of the change feed. - New ports Embedder and Index, with a deterministic local hashing embedder and an in-memory index. - Provenance on every chunk: tenant, cite, connector kind, source id, version, span, content SHA-256, ACL prefilter, release. Flags are empty until the sanitiser (SB-207).

LAW-13 has its first law test, red first, then green: ingest, re-ingest (no change), then drop and rebuild (same (cite, chunk, ACL) set).
