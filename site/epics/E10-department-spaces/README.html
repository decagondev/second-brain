E10: Department Spaces

A company's brain holds all its knowledge, split into department spaces that can only narrow what each source allows.

Waves: W10 · Tickets: 8 · Stories: 4

User stories

  • US-10.1: As a company IT operator, I want to declare our departments as spaces in the manifest, so that our brain mirrors how we're organised. Delivered by SB-1001, SB-1002, SB-1003.
  • US-10.2: As a company security lead, I want space policy that can only narrow what each source allows, so that adding a space can never leak anything. Delivered by SB-1004, SB-1005.
  • US-10.3: As a asker, I want to ask my department's brain or the whole company and see where each citation came from, so that I get the right answer from the right place. Delivered by SB-1006, SB-1007.
  • US-10.4: As a company IT operator, I want to audit and stop one space on its own, so that a problem in one department doesn't stop the company. Delivered by SB-1008.
