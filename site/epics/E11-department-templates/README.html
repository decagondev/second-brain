E11: Department Templates and Connectors

Every common department starts from a template with its sources, labels, agents and evals, including code, HR and accounting systems.

Waves: W11 · Tickets: 7 · Stories: 4

User stories

  • US-11.1: As a company IT operator, I want ready templates for common departments, so that we pick departments instead of designing them. Delivered by SB-1101, SB-1102.
  • US-11.2: As a engineer, I want our repositories, issues and pull requests in the Engineering brain, so that I can ask how our systems work. Delivered by SB-1103.
  • US-11.3: As a people or finance lead, I want our HR and accounting systems in our spaces with sensitive fields never ingested, so that we get answers without exposing pay, cases or bank details. Delivered by SB-1104, SB-1105, SB-1106.
  • US-11.4: As a maintainer, I want every template to pass its own suites, leak suites included, before it reaches a tenant, so that a template change can't quietly open a department. Delivered by SB-1107.
