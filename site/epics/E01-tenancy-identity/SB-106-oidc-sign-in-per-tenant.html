SB-106: OIDC sign-in per tenant

Why

Users sign in with the IdP they already have.

Acceptance criteria

  • ✓Login works with a test OIDC IdP
  • ✓Tokens carry tenant id; a token from tenant A is rejected by cell B
  • ✓Session lifetime and refresh follow sb-identity(7)

Subtasks

  • ✓Update the man pages listed above first (manual first, P2)
  • ✓OIDC adapter behind IdentityProvider port
  • ✓Session store per cell
  • ✓Two-IdP test fixture
  • ✓Write or extend the law tests: LAW-02
  • ✓Devlog entry, ledger and changelog updated

Progress

2026-11-19: built, standard library only. - internal/adapters/oidc does discovery, then verifies RS256 against the key set (refreshed at most once a minute), and checks issuer, audience, time with 60 seconds of leeway, nonce, tenant and subject. It runs the code flow with PKCE S256. oidctest is a TLS test IdP that also mints deliberately wrong tokens. - internal/core/session holds the session rules: 12 hours from sign-in with no extension, re-validated against ports.Directory every 60 seconds, and unknown or no answer ends the session. - internal/auth is the cell's /login and /callback, the session cookie, and Authenticate/TenantOf, which plugs into the SB-105 edge router. - internal/adapters/sessions/memory is the per-cell store. - Tests: a full browser sign-in against the test IdP, re-validation and 12-hour expiry, an unknown user refused, a two-IdP fixture (acme's session and acme's token both refused at beta), and one failing token per rule, including alg: none and a forged key. - LAW-02 gains a sign-in law test, red first, then green.

Left: the client secret is read through a function, and the vault adapter that backs it (vault://tenants/TENANT/oidc) comes with the secrets work. The directory is a fake until SCIM (SB-107). The production session store is the cell database. Also left: review by the user, and the first green CI run.
