E01: Tenancy and Identity

One manifest and one command make an isolated cell; users sign in with their own SSO and see only their tenant.

Waves: W1 · Tickets: 8 · Stories: 3

User stories

  • US-01.1: As a parent-org platform engineer, I want to add a company with one manifest and one pipeline run, so that onboarding is repeatable and boring. Delivered by SB-101, SB-102, SB-103, SB-104, SB-105.
  • US-01.2: As a company staff member, I want to sign in with my company's SSO and see only my company, so that I trust what I see. Delivered by SB-106, SB-108.
  • US-01.3: As a company IT operator, I want deprovisioning in our IdP to remove access within minutes, so that leavers lose access without a ticket to anyone. Delivered by SB-107.
