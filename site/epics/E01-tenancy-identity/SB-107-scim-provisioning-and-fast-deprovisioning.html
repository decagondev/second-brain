SB-107: SCIM provisioning and fast deprovisioning

Field Value
Epic E01 Tenancy and Identity
Wave / slice W1 / S1.3 Identity
Stories US-01.3
Manual sb-identity(7)
Laws LAW-12
Depends on SB-106
Status Done

Why

A leaver must lose access within minutes, not at the next sync.

Acceptance criteria

  • ✓SCIM create, update, deactivate supported
  • ✓Deactivate revokes sessions in 5 minutes or less (LAW-12 test)
  • ✓Group membership changes reach policy within the same bound

Subtasks

  • ✓Update the man pages listed above first (manual first, P2)
  • ✓SCIM endpoint per tenant
  • ✓Session revocation fan-out
  • ✓LAW-12 timed test
  • ✓Update ledger
  • ✓Write or extend the law tests: LAW-12
  • ✓Devlog entry, ledger and changelog updated

Progress

2026-11-19: built. - internal/scim serves SCIM 2.0 at /scim/v2 for the cell's tenant: Users (create, get, replace, patch, delete-as-deactivate) and Groups (create, replace, patch members, delete). It uses a bearer token compared in constant time, and accepts the "False" string some IdPs send. - Going from active to inactive revokes the user's sessions in the response path. A failed revocation is a 503, so the IdP retries. - ports.DirectoryStore adds users, groups and GroupsOf. Policy reads groups at decision time, so membership changes apply to the next decision. internal/adapters/directory/memory is the per-cell store for now.

LAW-12 has its first law test: red first, then green. It runs on a fake clock, with and without a lost revocation fan-out, and also requires that other users keep working. A cell that ignores the directory after a lost fan-out is caught.

2026-11-19: reviewed by the user; CI green. Done.
