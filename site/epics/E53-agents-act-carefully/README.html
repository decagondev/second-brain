E53: Agents That Act Carefully

Agents start on the work events that matter, can be tried before they're turned on, can borrow access for one run with a named lender, and draft the next action for a person to send.

Waves: W52 · Tickets: 6 · Stories: 2

Planned 2026-11-25 after W49, with the user. The concept is in 21-guard-write-act.md. Every ticket keeps to the rules there: no new service the cell must run, no new store, no new third-party Go module, at most one setting a feature, no new page, nothing widens what anyone sees, a person approves every write, the tree only narrows, agents are definitions in the release bundle, stored results are checked when read, every long run has a ceiling, one level of consultation, the AI label never comes off by itself, a rhythm only drafts and stops with its subject, a brief about a person draws only on what they published, and lent access lasts one run and travels with its result.

User stories

  • US-53.1: As an agent owner or lender, I want triggers on work events, a replay before they go live and access lent for one run, so that agents start at the right moment without seeing more than they should. Delivered by SB-5301, SB-5302, SB-5303.
  • US-53.2: As anyone, an admin or a meeting attendee, I want drafted next actions, managed MCP access and meeting transcripts from Drive, so that work moves on without the brain acting on its own. Delivered by SB-5304, SB-5305, SB-5306.

Exit gate

  • A work event starts one draft, floods batch, and nothing publishes without the approver
  • Lent access lasts one run, names the lender, and its result reaches only people who can open every cite (LAW-31 green)
  • Nothing is sent to Linear, Slack or Mattermost without the person's press
  • Every law test still green; no new store, service or Go module

Decisions for the user

Approved by the user on 2026-11-25 (21-guard-write-act.md), every recommended decision: borrowed access is included, one run at a time, with a named lender and the result limited to people who can open every cite (SB-5303, LAW-31); drafted actions go to Linear and Slack or Mattermost and are sent by the person with their own token (SB-5304); meeting transcripts come from Google Meet through the Drive connector, no new vendor (SB-5306).
