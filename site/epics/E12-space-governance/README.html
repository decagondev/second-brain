E12: Space Governance

Department leads run their own spaces; barriers, segregation of duties, access reviews and reorgs work the way a real organisation needs.

Waves: W12 · Tickets: 8 · Stories: 5

User stories

  • US-12.1: As a department steward, I want to run my space and re-certify its members without IT doing every change, so that my department's brain stays right and stays ours. Delivered by SB-1201, SB-1202.
  • US-12.2: As a compliance officer, I want information barriers and segregation of duties, so that deal teams, investigations and finance controls work as our policies say. Delivered by SB-1203, SB-1204.
  • US-12.3: As a people operations lead, I want reorgs to change access within 5 minutes, and to preview them first, so that a move never leaves someone with yesterday's access. Delivered by SB-1205, SB-1206.
  • US-12.4: As a legal lead, I want to put a space or scope on legal hold, so that nothing under a matter is deleted. Delivered by SB-1207.
  • US-12.5: As a company security lead, I want the spaces red-teamed before we rely on them, so that we trust the People and Finance brains. Delivered by SB-1208.
