E02: Policy Kernel

Every decision about who may see or do what goes through one small, analysable policy engine that treats unknown as deny.

Waves: W1 · Tickets: 4 · Stories: 3

User stories

  • US-02.1: As a security reviewer, I want one authorise call that every path goes through, so that there is one place to audit and prove. Delivered by SB-109.
  • US-02.2: As a company IT operator, I want policies written in a small, readable language with a schema, so that I can review rules without reading code. Delivered by SB-110, SB-112.
  • US-02.3: As an auditor, I want every policy decision recorded with its reason, so that I can explain any allow or deny later. Delivered by SB-111.
