# Returns policy

A customer may return an undamaged pallet within 30 days of delivery. The driver collects it
on the next scheduled run to that depot, at no charge.

Damaged goods are reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos attached to the delivery
ticket. Claims after 48 hours go to the claims team and take up to 10 working days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days of the pallet
arriving back at the depot.
